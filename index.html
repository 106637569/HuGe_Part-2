Building the Future of Digital Finance

HuGe is a financial technology company based in Melbourne. We design and maintain secure, user-friendly platforms for digital payments, personal budgeting, account management and financial education. Our products are used by individuals, students and small businesses who want to understand their money without needing a finance degree to do it.

We're a small team that cares about doing precise, well-tested work. The kind of work that makes people trust with their pay cheque. If that sounds like the sort of place you'd like to build your career, take a look at our current vacancies.

What we build

Payments

Fast, secure transfers between accounts, cards and digital wallets, built on top of bank-grade encryption.

Budgeting

Tools that turn transaction history into a plain-language picture of where money is going.

Financial education

Short, practical guides and calculators that explain concepts like interest and compounding in everyday language.
