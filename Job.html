Career Opportunities at HuGe

Join HuGe and help build the future of digital finance.

HuGe Financial Technologies develops secure and user-friendly digital solutions for payments, budgeting, account management and financial education.

We are looking for people who are interested in technology, finance and creating better digital experiences for customers.

Open Positions

Software Engineer

HGF101

Salary: AUD $90,000 - $110,000 per year + super

Reporting Line: Reports to the Engineering Manager

Short Description: Develop and maintain secure and scalable software solutions for HuGe's digital payment and financial platforms.

Key Responsibilities

  • Develop and test web-based financial applications.
  • Work with designers and other developers to create new features.
  • Maintain clean and readable code.
  • Identify and fix software problems.
  • Follow security and privacy requirements.

Essential Requirements

  • Bachelor degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering or a related field.
  • Knowledge of HTML, CSS and a programming language such as Java or Python.
  • Understanding of basic software development principles.
  • Good problem-solving skills.
  • Ability to work effectively in a team.

Preferable Requirements

  • Knowledge of cloud services or APIs.
  • Basic understanding of cybersecurity.
  • Previous experience with financial or banking software.

Data Analyst

HGA204

Salary: AUD $80,000 - $98,000 per year + super

Reporting Line: Reports to the Head of Product

Short Description: Analyse customer and business data to help HuGe improve its financial products and services.

Key Responsibilities

  • Collect and analyse customer and product data.
  • Prepare reports for managers and other teams.
  • Identify customer behaviour and usage trends.
  • Provide data to support business decisions.
  • Help improve HuGe's financial products.

Essential Requirements

  • Degree in Data Analytics, Business, Information Systems or a related field.
  • Ability to understand and interpret data.
  • Knowledge of spreadsheets and databases.
  • Good communication skills.
  • Strong attention to detail.

Preferable Requirements

  • Experience using SQL or Python.
  • Experience working with financial data.
  • Knowledge of data visualisation tools.
